## 1. Proof Statement

> I am an ML intern who built and deployed production-grade baseline action models
> and rigorous data contracts for a search-intelligence startup — proven in a public
> repository where you can run the pipeline and read the receipts. I wrote this for
> the engineering leader or hiring manager who is one bad hire away from trusting a
> candidate's claims, and the only action I ask for is an email to talk about where
> I could do this kind of work on their team.

### The three elements, made explicit

| Element | What it is | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **The one primary claim** | Building **production-grade baseline action models** and **rigorous data contracts** — a named, verifiable ML skill, not a generic "data-savvy" line | ML-07 baseline action model; ML-04 data contracts |
| **The one specific person** | An **engineering leader or hiring manager who can hire me** — a reader with the authority to turn an email into a role | Written to this reader directly ("for the engineering leader or hiring manager…") |
| **The one action** | **Email me for opportunities** | The only call-to-action on the site; everything routes back to it (see sitemap audit) |

### Why this is narrow and distinct

- It names **one skill pair** (action models + data contracts), not a menu of everything I touched.
- It names **one reader** with hiring power, not "anyone who stumbles on my site."
- It asks for **one action** (email), not three competing ones (follow, download resume, connect on LinkedIn).
- "Production-grade" is a deliberate, evidence-backed word: it is only kept because the
  repository ships runnable code, validation receipts, and observed metrics to carry it —
  per the claim ladder in `skills/writing-honest-claims/SKILL.md`, the language never
  exceeds the evidence.

## 2. One-Line "Why"

> A CV lists what I did; only a public portfolio can show a skeptical engineer that
> I actually did it — pipeline, leakage controls, and metrics they can verify themselves.

A CV and LinkedIn assert. They cannot be run, inspected, or re-validated. This portfolio's
reason to exist is that it **demonstrates** the claim instead of asserting it — which is
exactly the thing a resume alone can never prove.
